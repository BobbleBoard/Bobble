Words in a picture: rounded lines standing in for text, so a picture shows the shape of a page without anything to read.

Wrap lines in `.bb-lines`; each `.bb-line` takes `--w` for its width. Lines are 8 high on gaps of 8, groups are 20 apart, and the last line of a group is short. `--strong` marks a heading line, `--hue` a link; `.bb-selected` on a group tints each line like selected text.

- Do give every picture one real title and draw the rest as lines.
- Do not put lorem ipsum or tiny real text in a picture.
