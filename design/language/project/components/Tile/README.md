The unit everything in a picture is built from: the mark's own square, with a corner of 28% of its side.

Use `.bb-tile` with `--s` for the side; `--tint`, `--ink`, `--line` and `--lift` change the fill. `.bb-dot` is a tile rounded to 50%, `.bb-pill` a dot stretched by `--w`. Because the corner is a share of the side (`radius-tile`), a tile is the same shape from 8px to 168px.

- Do build buttons, marks, swatches and markers from tiles.
- Do not use plain circles, squares with small radii, or blobs in a picture.
