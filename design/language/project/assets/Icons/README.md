The app's own icons, rendered from packages/ui (glyph.tsx and icons.tsx), stroke only, ink `#1D1D1F`.

- `glyph-*.svg`: the 24-grid Hugeicons set (MIT, see Licenses), stroke 1.5 at 24px, round caps and joins. Places and tools: sidebar rows, studios, menu items.
- `icon-*.svg`: the 16-grid control set, stroke 1.5 at 16px. Check, close, copy, share, pencil, chevrons, and the rest of a control's vocabulary.

Inline them with `stroke="currentColor"` to take the text colour; as files they are ink-only. No brand marks are here: a connector's icon is its official mark, copied separately.
